Research

Modular agentic systems, statistical analysis and reproducible pipelines for behavioural and urban data.

Research Assistant

Malmö University, Department of Technology and Society · Sweden · 2024 — present

  • Research on AI-assisted analysis of travel behaviour, mobility choices and urban planning data, including large-scale surveys and demographic differences.
  • Development of Python pipelines for data cleaning, feature selection, statistical analysis, model-supported reasoning, retrieval and report generation.
  • Design of modular agentic systems with specialised components for preprocessing, analysis, classification, validation and reporting.
  • Use of structured interaction traces, shared workflow states, validation checkpoints and intermediate outputs to support interpretability, auditing and reproducibility.
  • Contribution to study design, expert and participant protocols, rating instruments, statistical validation and transparent reporting of limitations.
  • Development of retrieval-augmented systems for urban planning and mobility decision support; co-supervision of a master's thesis on multimodal RAG for travel behaviour analysis.

Master's thesis

Leveraging LLMs for Understanding Travel Behaviour

A case study on older adults in Malmö

Malmö University · 2025 · grade A

  • Design and implementation of a multi-agent system based on foundation models for large-scale travel behaviour analysis, with structured JSON handovers between specialised agents.
  • Evaluation of the system in a blind study with six domain experts, compared with a commercial single-agent system and a baseline written by human experts.
  • Analysis of correctness, reasoning, relevance and clarity ratings using one-way ANOVA and Tukey HSD post-hoc tests.
  • Grade A, Thesis of the Year award and Wollmarska fonden scholarship 2026; the work was developed into a peer-reviewed paper.
